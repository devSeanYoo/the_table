# THE TABLE — Country Cards (Final, B1 English)

Each team sees only their own country's card. Resource holdings, passives, and special moves are private info — only visible on this card, not on the public dashboard.

---

## 🇪🇹 ETHIOPIA

*Your river feeds a whole region. Other countries need your water — but you need to grow, too.*

**Resources you make every round:** 3 Water, 1 Food

**Passive (always on):** Bad water news never hurts you. If an event is about water, you are safe.

**Special Move — Water Control** *(use up to 3 times)*
- Press this to get +2 Water right now.
- At the same time, Egypt loses all its Water and Power this round.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇪🇬 EGYPT

*Your country farms next to a great river. But that water starts in another country — not yours.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Oil, 1 Gas, 1 Water, 1 Power

**Passive (always on):** Bad oil news hits you harder. If an event is about oil, the bad effect lasts one extra round for you.

**Special Move — Stop Ethiopia** *(use 1 time)*
- Press this if Ethiopia uses "Water Control" against you.
- This cancels the whole move — Ethiopia gets nothing, and you take no damage.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇱🇦 LAOS

*Your rivers make strong power. Thailand needs some of it too.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Water, 3 Power

**Passive (always on):** Every round, this happens without you doing anything: you give 1 Power to Thailand. You still keep 2 Power for yourself.

**Special Move — Emergency Release** *(use up to 2 times)*
- Press this to stop giving power to Thailand and get +2 Power right now.
- Thailand gets zero Power this round.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇹🇭 THAILAND

*You grow more rice than almost anyone. But your lights stay on because of your neighbor, Laos.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Gas, 1 Water, 3 Food (plus Power from Laos, if they keep sending it)

**Passive (always on):** Bad food news hits you harder. If an event is about food, the bad effect lasts one extra round for you.

**Special Move — Backup Plan** *(use 1 time)*
- Press this if Laos uses "Emergency Release" against you.
- This blocks the damage to you. Laos still keeps their own gain.
- No one sees this — it stays secret.

---

## 🇹🇲 TURKMENISTAN *(1-person team)*

*You sit on one of the biggest gas fields in the world. But you can't get it out of the ground yet.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Oil, 1 Gas

**Passive (always on):** Right now you only make 1 Gas per round. The first time you collect a total of 3 Minerals (from one or more trades), you use them to build new gas pipelines. Starting next round, you make 4 Gas every round for the rest of the game.

**Special Move — Block It** *(use 1 time)*
- Press this to cancel one Special Move used against you.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇻🇪 VENEZUELA

*You have more oil underground than any other country here. But it's heavy oil — hard to sell without power.*

**Resources you make every round:** 3 Oil, 1 Gas, 1 Water

**Passive (always on):** You need at least 1 Power every round to trade your Oil. Without Power, you can still use the Oil yourself — you just can't sell it.

**Special Move — Claim New Land** *(use 1 time)*
- Press this to get +3 Oil from outside the game. No other country loses anything.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇧🇴 BOLIVIA

*You have huge amounts of minerals. But you have no coast — everything you send out loses a little on the way.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Gas, 3 Minerals

**Passive (always on):** Every time you send resources to another country, they receive 1 less than you sent (never below 0).

**Special Move — Take It Back** *(use 1 time)*
- Press this to cancel your most recent trade and get your resources back.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇺🇦 UKRAINE

*Your fields feed half the world. But right now, sending food to other places is not easy.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Mineral, 3 Food

**Passive (always on):** You can only trade Food in groups of 3 or more. Smaller trades are not allowed.

**Special Move — Special Deal** *(use 1 time)*
- Press this to remove the 3-unit rule for this round only. You can trade any amount of Food.
- Everyone will see the news when you use this.

---

## 🇨🇦 CANADA

*You have a little bit of everything. You'll need to keep trading, too.*

**Resources you make every round:** 1 Oil, 1 Gas, 1 Mineral, 1 Water, 1 Food, 1 Power *(all six!)*

**Passive:** None. Maple syrup on everything, eh? 🍁🥞

**Special Move — Quick Swap** *(use up to 3 times)*
- Press this to trade 1 of your resources for 1 of any other resource, instantly. No other country is involved.
- No one sees this — it stays secret.
