# THE TABLE — Events (Final, B1 English)

Events happen on the public dashboard. Everyone sees them at the same time. Round 1 has no event.

---

## EVENT — Round 2: Mekong Drought

*The river is running low. Dams can't make power like before.*

**What happens:**
- Laos makes 0 Power this round (Thailand gets 0 too — no power comes through).
- Thailand's Water drops by 1 this round.

---

## EVENT — Round 3: Oil Route Blocked

*Ships full of oil can't get through. Trouble at sea is stopping them.*

**What happens:**
- Venezuela, Egypt, Turkmenistan, and Canada cannot trade Oil this round.
- You can still use your own Oil at home — you just can't sell or buy it.

---

## EVENT — Round 4: Clean Energy Boom

*Solar panels and wind farms are going up fast.*

**What happens:**
- Canada and Laos make +1 Power every round from now on. This is permanent.

---

## EVENT — Round 5: Food Prices Spike

*Food costs more everywhere. Everyone needs more just to get by.*

**What happens:**
- Every country needs 1 more Food than usual this round.

---

## EVENT — Round 6: Rising Expectations 🔴 *(this always happens once, timing is a surprise)*

*People want a better life. Governments raise the bar.*

**What happens:**
- Ethiopia, Egypt, Thailand, and Canada now need more resources every round, for the rest of the game:
  - Energy: 3 (was 2)
  - Water: 2 (was 1)
  - Food: 2 (was 1)
- This is permanent. Other countries are not affected.

---

## EVENT — Round 7: Energy Price War

*Oil and gas sellers are fighting for buyers. Prices are a mess.*

**What happens:**
- Any Oil or Gas trade this round follows a new rule: the seller must send 2 for the buyer to receive 1.
- This rule cannot be talked around — it applies no matter what you agree to.

---

## EVENT — Round 8: Global Drought Warning

*Dry weather is hitting the whole world at once.*

**What happens:**
- Every country needs 1 more Water than usual this round.

---

## EVENT — Round 9: The Climate Deal *(a group choice — announced now, decided at the end of the round)*

*World leaders want a deal on oil and gas. Will everyone agree — or will someone cheat?*

**What happens:**
- Trade Oil and Gas as much as you want this round, like normal.
- Right before the round ends, every country secretly picks one:
  - **Comply** — you agree not to use the Oil and Gas you collected this round to meet your Energy need.
  - **Ignore** — you use your Oil and Gas normally, like any other round.
- If **6 or more countries Comply**: every country gets **+3 points** — even the ones who picked Ignore. Ignore is the best choice here: you get the bonus AND keep using your resources.
- If **5 or fewer countries Comply**: no bonus for anyone. Countries that picked Comply also miss out on their Energy need this round.

---

## EVENT — Round 10: Battery Boom

*Phones, cars, batteries — the whole world wants Minerals right now.*

**What happens:**
- Every country gets **+1 point for every Mineral they currently have**, right away.
- This is a one-time bonus. It does not use up your Minerals.
